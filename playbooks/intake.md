# Playbook: Normalize Intake

Read: [`standards/lifecycle.md`](../standards/lifecycle.md), [`standards/bug.md`](../standards/bug.md)

Capture first. A Jira item at intake is not approved, prioritized, ready or committed.

## Steps

1. Understand the request and keep useful source evidence.
2. Search Jira for duplicates, related work and prior requirements.
3. Decide what it represents: Bug (`playbooks/triage-bug.md`), Story, Task, Spike, Epic, reporting need, process issue, or configuration/data issue.
4. Identify the underlying need. Keep the requested solution as input; challenge it and recommend a better one when warranted.
5. Normalize raw forms, threads, screenshots and payloads into an actionable description. Never copy secrets.
6. Work toward Business Requirements Complete (`standards/lifecycle.md`); confirm the business supplies priority and due date.
7. For a Story, continue with `playbooks/write-story.md`. Create or update Jira only within the user's authorization.

## Output

- Underlying need; requested and recommended solution
- Classification and related Jira work
- Business Requirements Complete gaps
- Recommended action and draft content, when useful
