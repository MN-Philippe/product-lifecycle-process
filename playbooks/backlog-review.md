# Playbook: Backlog Review

## Goal

Prepare a decision-oriented view of live Jira work without turning the review into a giant ticket-by-ticket status meeting.

## Steps

1. Query live Jira for the relevant owner, product, initiative, release, or time window.
2. Surface newly created work, orphaned work, stale work, major scope changes, and hierarchy inconsistencies.
3. Group work by outcome/initiative where possible rather than listing individual tickets first.
4. Identify conflicts, dependencies, ownership gaps, and priority collisions.
5. Distinguish decisions that can be made by one owner from cross-functional tradeoffs that need broader leadership alignment.
6. Highlight what changed since the last relevant decision point when that history is available.
7. Produce a concise decision/action list.

## Output

Prefer:

- **What changed**
- **New work needing ownership/context**
- **Conflicts/dependencies**
- **Roadmap/release impacts**
- **Decisions needed**
- **Actions / owners**

Product/PM facilitates the shared view. Business/ELT owners own their requests and priorities. Cross-functional tradeoffs belong to the group that has authority to make them.
