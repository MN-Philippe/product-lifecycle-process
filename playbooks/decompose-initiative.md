# Playbook: Decompose an Initiative

## Goal

Break a finite outcome into coherent delivery work without losing the business intent or prematurely over-specifying implementation.

## Steps

1. Fetch the Epic/initiative live and inspect current children.
2. Confirm the problem, desired outcome, scope, constraints, and important non-scope.
3. Identify major user/business capabilities and technical enablers required to reach the outcome.
4. Separate product behavior from engineering/platform work.
5. Identify dependencies, sequencing constraints, migration needs, observability/testing needs, and rollout concerns.
6. Propose Stories/Tasks/Spikes that are independently understandable and have clear completion criteria.
7. Avoid decomposition that merely mirrors components or teams unless that is genuinely how value must be delivered.
8. Recommend durable artifacts only where the initiative needs design/decision material beyond Jira.

## Output

- **Outcome and scope recap**
- **Proposed work breakdown**
- **Dependencies / sequencing**
- **Open decisions**
- **Recommended Jira changes**
- **Recommended artifacts**, if any

Do not create all proposed Jira work unless authorized.
